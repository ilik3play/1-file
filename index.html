Mashup Studio

Put the vocals from one song over the beat from another, or mix sections from several songs together. Your song files stay on your device: nothing is uploaded. iTunes searches go to Apple and lyrics lookups go to LRCLIB.

1. Songs

Add audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG), or search iTunes for 30-second previews. Each song's tempo and key are detected automatically. Use Split vocals & music to separate the singing from the instruments.

2. Mashup

Drag across a song's waveform to pick a section, then press + Beat or + Vocals. Drag sections on the timeline to move them, and drag their edges to trim them.
